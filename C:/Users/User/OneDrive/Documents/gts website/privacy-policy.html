Legal & Security

Privacy Policy

At Global Title Services, data confidentiality and system security are paramount. This policy outlines how we protect and manage your data.

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Introduction

Global Title Services ("GTS", "we", "us", or "our") operates a secure data and title information management platform. We are committed to maintaining the privacy and security of all client records, property information, and communication transmitted across our computing systems.

2. Information We Collect

In performing title searches, tax reports, document typing, and digital services, we may collect:

  • Contact details (name, email address, phone number, company organization).
  • Real property data (property address, parcel IDs, county records, legal descriptions).
  • System usage data and IP addresses for security auditing and access authorization.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected data solely to fulfill service orders, process tax certificates, prepare ALTA-compliant title commitments, communicate status updates, and ensure operational availability. We do not sell or monetize client data.

4. Data Protection & Security

We possess and operate a secure, private, and scalable computing infrastructure monitored 24/7. All sensitive records are encrypted during transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). Access is restricted to authorized personnel governed by strict confidentiality policies.
