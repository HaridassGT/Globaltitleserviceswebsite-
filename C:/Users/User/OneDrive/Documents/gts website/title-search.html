Service Line 01

Title Search & Abstracting

Detailed property title searches and abstracting services for title agencies, underwriters, and real estate professionals across the United States.

Complete Record Integrity

Precise abstracting from county land records.

Our abstracting team retrieves and verifies public records, deeds, mortgages, liens, easements, and tax information across all US jurisdictions.

  • 01
    Current Owner Search

    Examines the title from the vesting deed to the present date, capturing open mortgages, liens, tax status, and judgments.

  • 02
    Two Owner Search

    Traces title back through the prior two warranty deeds to verify chain of title continuity and clear ownership history.

  • 03
    Full Search

    Full 30 to 60-year statutory title search covering all historical conveyances, restrictions, covenants, and easements.

  • 04
    Legal & Vesting Report

    Verifies property legal descriptions, metes and bounds, tax map numbers, and current vesting party names.

  • 05
    Assignment Verification Report (AVR)

    Traces security instrument assignment chains to confirm mortgage ownership and legal standing.

  • 06
    Lien & Judgment Search

    Searches state and federal tax liens, mechanic's liens, civil judgments, and bankruptcy filings against all title holders.
